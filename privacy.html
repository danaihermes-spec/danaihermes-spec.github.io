Privacy policy

Hermes — a private personal assistant used by one person, its owner (Danai).

What data Hermes accesses

With the owner's Google account, and only after the owner grants consent, Hermes accesses:

  • Google Calendar — read and create/modify events on the owner's own calendars.
  • Gmail — read and modify the owner's own messages.
  • Google Tasks — read and create/modify the owner's own tasks.

How the data is used

The data is used only to assist the owner — for example to show him his own schedule, to add an event or a task he asked for, and to summarise his own mail. Nothing else.

Sharing

The data is not sold and not shared with third parties. Hermes has no other users and no advertising.

Storage

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. There is no shared server and no database operated for other people.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Hermes' access to his Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revoking, Hermes can no longer read or change anything in the account.
